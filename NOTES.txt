Kenya - published guidance for officials deciding immigration, citizenship and refugee cases
Checked 2026-11-15. Re-run: python3 download_ke.py (from the folder above this one).

Result in one paragraph
Kenya publishes no guidance written for its decision-makers. The Directorate of Immigration
Services publishes service charters, application checklists and forms; the Department of
Refugee Services publishes laws, a service guide and FAQs for refugees, and plans and reports.
What exists of the working rules is in legislation. Two procedural acts were saved: the
Refugees (General) Regulations 2024, which lay down reception, registration and the refugee
status determination process applied by the Department, and the rules of procedure of the
refugee appeal body (Refugees (Appeal Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2017, in a reviewed text
published by the body itself). The Kenya Citizenship and Immigration Regulations 2012, which
govern permits and passes, could not be obtained: the only official online text is on Kenya
Law, whose robots.txt excludes AI agents.

Sources checked
- https://immigration.go.ke/ (robots.txt allows the site; it also carries injected spam links):
  home page, work permits and passes pages, PDF media list (/wp-json/wp/v2/media: two
  service charters), and the web archive's list of PDFs on the domain since 2006 (checklists,
  forms, passport delivery lists, questionnaires, the Act). fns.immigration.go.ke (no
  robots.txt) appears only in the archive with checklists.
- https://refugee.go.ke/ (Department of Refugee Services; robots.txt allows /sites/): sitemap,
  Acts/Regulations/Policies, Downloads, Resources, Service Charter, RSD and Documentation
  pages. Documents are the laws, the 2024 Regulations, Shirika Plan papers, the Refugee Service
  Guide (2026, for applicants), FAQs, camp-visit permit instructions and notices.
- https://refugeeappeals.go.ke/ (robots.txt allows /sites/): home, Downloads, Appeal Process.
- https://borders.etakenya.go.ke/ (no robots.txt): border management fact sheets and the 2023
  amendment to the 2012 Regulations (ETA, API/PNR; left out, see not-obtained.tsv).
- https://interior.go.ke/: robots.txt address returns the site's HTML shell; not used further.
- https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/: its pages on the 2012 Regulations link only to Kenya Law.
