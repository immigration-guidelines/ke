# Immigration guidance: Kenya

Published guidance that the authorities of Kenya issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** 5 files in this folder. `index.tsv` lists each file with its title, date and source address.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` and `not-obtained.tsv` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

Kenya publishes no procedure manuals, work-permit guidelines or refugee status determination procedures for its officials. This repository holds the two sets of refugee regulations that set out reception, registration, status determination and appeals, marked `procedural act`. The Kenya Citizenship and Immigration Regulations 2012 are published online only on Kenya Law, whose robots.txt excludes AI agents.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
